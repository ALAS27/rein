Letter

Dear ate Indel, happy 19th birthday. I wanted this message to sound formal enough for the occasion, but still honest enough to feel like me, so please accept this carefully prepared birthday speech with a small amount of emotional damage.

It is funny how fast time moves. One moment, the memories are just normal days: laughing over random things, talking about crushes like they were breaking news, and spending time around Children's Ministry. Then suddenly, those same moments become the kind of memories you look back on and quietly smile about.

I hope you know that you are appreciated not only for the big things people notice, but also for the small things that make you you. Your kindness, your faith, your strength, and even your very specific way of making ordinary moments more entertaining all matter more than you probably realize.

This year, I pray that God gives you peace when life feels loud, courage when things feel uncertain, and joy that stays even in simple days. May your prayers be answered in ways that surprise you, and may every delay eventually make sense in the most beautiful way.

Keep growing, keep dreaming, and keep becoming the person God is shaping you to be. We are proud of you, ate Indel. And yes, this is your reminder that you are loved, prayed for, and celebrated today, even if this website is doing most of the dramatic work.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future." Jeremiah 29:11
